# Issue 04: DeathSystem rewrite with Commander promotion + kill point awards + tests

## Parent

`.scratch/v1-design-overhaul/PRD.md`

## What to build

Rewrite DeathSystem to handle Commander promotion and kill point tracking. When the Commander entity dies (HP <= 0 and has CommanderComponent with IsCommander=true), find the highest-level CombatUnit in the same Squad (by SquadID), promote it to Commander (set IsCommander=true, add CommanderComponent), and keep its CombatUnitType. The Squad may become mixed-type after promotion.

Track dead entities in a DeathLog for the persistence layer. Each death records the entity's SquadID, CombatUnitType, level, and whether it was a Commander. This log is consumed by the persistence flush to update the roster.

Kill point awards happen here (not in CombatSystem) — when an entity dies, the killer's UnitTypeComponent.KillPoints is incremented. Gold bounty is awarded in CombatSystem on the damage-dealing tick; kill points are awarded here on the death-processing tick.

Remove the old handleCommanderDeath Boid weight adjustment (cohesion/separation tweaking). Instead, implement the promotion logic.
