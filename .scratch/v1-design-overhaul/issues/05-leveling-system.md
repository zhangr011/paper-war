# Issue 05: LevelingSystem + tests

## Parent

`.scratch/v1-design-overhaul/PRD.md`

## What to build

Create a new ECS system (LevelingSystem) that checks kill point thresholds and applies level-ups. Runs after DeathSystem in the tick pipeline so that kill points awarded by DeathSystem are processed in the same tick.

CombatUnit leveling (max 6): exponential thresholds. Cumulative kill points needed: 2 (level 2), 6 (level 3), 14 (level 4), 30 (level 5), 62 (level 6). Each level grants +10% HP and +10% damage (multiplicative). A level 6 unit has 1.5x HP and 1.5x damage compared to level 1.

Commander leveling (max 10): same exponential thresholds extended. Cumulative kill points: 2, 6, 14, 30, 62, 126, 254, 510, 1022. Commander stats scale using a multiplier table, NOT +10% per level. HP multiplier: 2x (level 1) → 5x (level 10). Damage multiplier: 1.5x (level 1) → 3x (level 10). Base stats come from CombatUnitType, multiplied by the level scaling factor.

Leading Skill growth: every 3 kill points earned by the Commander increments Leading Skill by 1 (max 50). This is checked and applied here.

Level-ups are applied immediately — HP is increased, MaxHP is increased, damage on AttackComponent is recalculated.
