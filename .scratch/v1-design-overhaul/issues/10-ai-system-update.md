## What to build

Update the AI system to work with the new type system, recruitment, and objectives.

AI behavior:
- Random Commander type: on match start, AI gets a random CombatUnitType from the 7 types (weighted toward simpler types)
- Smart targeting: AI units use the same smart auto-targeting as player units (priority tiers via Damage Matrix)
- Balanced recruitment: AI recruits proportionally to its Formation Template slots (e.g., if template is 4 Light Infantry + 2 Motor Artillery, recruit in 2:1 ratio)
- AI recruits when it can afford the cheapest available unit type and has Formation Template slots open
- Patrol near objective: on Capture maps, AI Commander patrols within 5 tiles of the target stronghold
- AI fights normally on Survival and Elimination maps (standard chase-and-attack behavior)
- AI never uses AttackGround (no terrain destruction)
- AI earns and spends Gold using the same economy as the player
