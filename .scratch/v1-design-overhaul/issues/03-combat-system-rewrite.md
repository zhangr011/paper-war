# Issue 03: CombatSystem rewrite with type-aware damage, splash, smart targeting + tests

## Parent

`.scratch/v1-design-overhaul/PRD.md`

## What to build

Rewrite CombatSystem to use the 4x3 Damage Matrix for all damage calculations. Remove the old melee/ranged/artillery attack type distinction. Implement Cannon splash (2-tile radius, 100%/50%/25% falloff). Implement smart auto-targeting with 4 priority tiers (150% > 100% > 50% > 25%). Remove all projectile spawning — all damage is instant in v1.

Key changes:

- Each attacking entity looks up its weapon from UnitTypeComponent, looks up the target's armor from UnitTypeComponent, applies DamageMultiplier to base damage
- Cannon-type attacks: after dealing primary damage, scan SpatialHash for entities within 2 tiles of the target, apply splash falloff (50% at 1 tile, 25% at 2 tiles), skip the primary target
- Smart targeting: on target acquisition, scan nearby enemies via SpatialHash, group by damage multiplier (using attacker's weapon vs each enemy's armor), pick closest enemy from highest tier group
- Units skip 25% targets unless no other enemy exists
- On kill: award Gold bounty to the killer's Commander (stored on UnitTypeComponent.Gold), award kill points to the killer's UnitTypeComponent.KillPoints

## Acceptance criteria
